Welcome to our engineering notebook

It works like a real book. Here are the only five things you need to know.

  1. 1
    Turn the page
    Swipe across the page, or click Prev / Next, or press the arrow keys.
  2. 2
    Zoom in for detail
    Double-click a page, or use + / − in the corner. Then drag (or middle-drag) to move around.
  3. 3
    Jump anywhere
    The bar along the bottom is the whole notebook. Every bookmark is marked on it — drag the bar, or click a marker.
  4. 4
    Mark it up
    Highlight or Pen on the right, then drag across the page. Your marks stay on your device only.
  5. 5
    Save your spot
    Bookmark drops your own marker onto the bottom bar so you can come back.

How to use this notebook

Turn pages

Click Prev/Next, drag a page corner, scroll the mouse wheel, or drag the bar along the bottom.

The bottom bar

It stands for the whole notebook, start to finish. Every bookmark sits on it at its page, so you can see the shape of the notebook at a glance. Hover anywhere along it to preview the page there — keep hovering as you drag — and click to jump.

Go to a page

Type the number in the box at the top and press Enter, or open All pages.

Finding your way back

A bookmark, a search result or a link can drop you a long way from where you were. Go back in the left rail returns you to the page you jumped from, and keeps stepping back through earlier jumps.

Zoom in

Use + / − in the corner of the page, double-click a page, or hold Ctrl and scroll. Zoomed in, drag the page — or hold the middle mouse button — to move around.

Links work

Contents entries and links inside the notebook are clickable, just like the paper version. Hover one to preview where it goes.

Highlight & draw

Pick Highlight or Pen from the right-hand rail, choose a colour and a thickness, then drag on the page. Erase removes marks, Undo steps back.

Your marks are private

Everything you draw or bookmark is saved in your own browser. Nobody else sees it and the notebook itself never changes.

Search

Search looks through every word in the notebook and lists the pages it found.

Keyboard shortcuts

←→ turn pages +− zoom 0 fit page H highlight P pen E erase B bookmark F fullscreen L light / dark Ctrl+Z undo Ctrl+Y redo Esc close / stop drawing
I’m the author — how do I add bookmarks everyone sees?

Turn on author mode. The bookmarks you add then appear for every visitor instead of only for you. When you’re done, click Save bookmarks file: your browser downloads bookmarks.json. Put that file in this website’s folder, replacing the old one, and re-upload — that’s it.
